03 / Approach

How we work

Privacy by design

Privacy is part of the first sketch, not a patch at the end. We collect as little as possible, and with Bookmark+ nothing at all.

Built in the open

Ideas, progress and mistakes are shared with a community of people who like to build and learn together.

Small and focused

Each app does a few things well, and works without an account or a server.
