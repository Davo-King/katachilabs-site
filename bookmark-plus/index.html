What it does

Everything in one calm place.

01

Organise

Group bookmarks into colour-coded categories laid out in columns. Pin your must-have sites and let Favourites show your most used.

02

Find fast

Search as you type across names, sites and projects. Press / to jump to search, or Enter to search the web with your own default search engine.

03

Save projects

Save the current page, or all the web pages open in your current window, into a project with Alt+Shift+B, then open the whole project again later.

  • Import your Chrome bookmarks in one step; Bookmark+ only reads them
  • Back up and restore with a JSON file
  • Light and dark mode, with six colour themes
  • Undo for deletes and backup restores
Private by design

Your bookmarks never leave your device.

Bookmark+ has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Data is kept in Chrome's local extension storage only. Because nothing is sent anywhere, bookmarks do not sync between computers on their own: use Download backup on one and Restore from backup on the other.
