Bookmark+ Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Bookmark+ Chrome extension ("Bookmark+", "the extension"), made by KatachiLabs, handles your information. Published at https://katachilabs.uk/bookmark-plus/privacy/

The short version

  • Bookmark+ does not collect, transmit or sell any of your data.
  • Everything you save (bookmarks, categories, projects and settings) is stored locally on your device in Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • KatachiLabs never sees your bookmarks or browsing activity.

What data Bookmark+ handles

Bookmark+ handles only the information you choose to put into it:

  • Bookmarks you save (page titles, web addresses and any notes you add), categories, pins, favourites and projects.
  • Web addresses and titles of pages you choose to save: the page you are on, the web pages open in your current window when you use Save All Tabs, or your Chrome bookmarks when you import them. The Chrome Web Store calls this kind of information "web history". Bookmark+ keeps it only in local storage on your device and never sends it to KatachiLabs or anyone else. In this policy, "collect" means KatachiLabs receiving your data, which Bookmark+ never does.
  • How often you open each bookmark, so Favourites can show your most-used sites.
  • Your settings (for example theme, layout and column widths) and a note of when you last made a backup.
  • A temporary copy of your previous data when you import a backup file, so the import can be undone.

This data is used only to show and organise your dashboard. It never leaves your device unless you choose to download a backup yourself.

When you open a bookmark, your browser visits that site as it normally would, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

Where your data is stored

  • Local storage only. Data is kept in chrome.storage.local on the computer where Bookmark+ is installed. Bookmark+ does not use Chrome Sync storage (chrome.storage.sync), so your data is not copied to your Google account or to other devices by the extension.
  • Backups are yours. If you use Download backup, Bookmark+ creates a JSON file that you save wherever you choose. If you use Restore from backup, it reads a file you select. KatachiLabs never receives these files.
  • Removing your data. Uninstalling Bookmark+ deletes its stored data from Chrome. Any backup files you exported remain where you saved them until you delete them.

Permissions and why they are needed

Permission Why Bookmark+ needs it
storage Saves your bookmarks, categories, projects and settings locally on your device.
favicon Shows each bookmark's website icon from Chrome's built-in icon cache, without contacting the websites or any third party. Chrome describes this as "Read the icons of the websites that you visit".
activeTab When you click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+B, reads the title and address of the current tab so you can save it. Only that tab, only when you ask.
search When you search the web from the dashboard, sends your query to your own default search engine through Chrome. That search engine's own privacy policy applies.
bookmarks (optional) Asked for only when you choose "Import Chrome bookmarks". Reads your Chrome bookmarks once to copy them into the dashboard, and is released afterwards. Bookmark+ never changes your Chrome bookmarks.
tabs (optional) Asked for only when you first use "Save all tabs". Reads the titles and addresses of tabs in the current window so you can save them as a project.

Bookmark+ requests no host permissions, runs no remote code, and its content security policy blocks loading scripts from anywhere other than the extension itself.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. Because Bookmark+ does not collect data, there is nothing to share. Bookmark+ does not use your data for advertising, credit-worthiness or lending, or any purpose unrelated to organising your bookmarks.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Chrome APIs by Bookmark+ adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Bookmark+ is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

As KatachiLabs does not hold any of your personal data from Bookmark+, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete, as you are in full control of the data on your device. If you have questions, contact us below. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Bookmark+ changes how data is handled (for example an optional cloud backup, or a paid option), we will update this page, change the "Last updated" date and, for material changes, tell you in the extension before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@katachilabs.uk

If you email us, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list. We keep the email for as long as we need it to help you, then delete it.
