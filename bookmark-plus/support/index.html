Bookmark+ support

How can we help?

Answers to the questions we expect most, and how to reach a real person if yours is not covered.

Contact us

Email contact@katachilabs.uk. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

It helps if you tell us what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and which version of Chrome you use (in Chrome, open the menu, then Help, then About Google Chrome). Please do not send passwords or your backup file unless we ask for it.

If you email us, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list. We keep the email for as long as we need it to help you, then delete it. See the privacy policy for how Bookmark+ itself handles data.

Common questions

Where are my bookmarks stored?

Only on your own computer, in Chrome's local extension storage. There is no account and no server, so we never see your bookmarks and cannot look up or recover them for you. That is also why a backup file is worth keeping (see below).

How do I back up my bookmarks, or move them to another computer?

Open Settings (the gear icon) and choose Download backup. Keep the file somewhere safe. On the other computer, install Bookmark+, open Settings and choose Restore from backup, then pick the file.

Restoring replaces what is on that computer with the contents of the file. You will be asked to confirm first, and you can undo straight afterwards. Bookmark+ does not sync between computers on its own.

I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for a few seconds. After you delete a bookmark, category or project, a message appears with an Undo button. Once it has gone, the delete cannot be undone, so a recent backup is your safety net. Importing Chrome bookmarks has no undo button either; if you want to be able to step back, download a backup first.

How do I bring in my Chrome bookmarks?

Choose Import Chrome bookmarks on the welcome screen or in Settings. Chrome will ask for permission to read your bookmarks. Bookmark+ only reads them, never changes them, and gives the permission back when it has finished. Each folder becomes a category, and importing again skips pages you already have.

Why does Bookmark+ ask to see my tabs?

Only the first time you use Save All Tabs in the toolbar popup. It then reads the titles and addresses of the web pages (http and https) open in your current window, so you can save them together as a project. Other pages, such as Chrome's own settings pages, cannot be saved. Saving just the page you are on does not need this permission.

Why does Chrome say "Read the icons of the websites that you visit"?

This is how Chrome describes the permission that lets Bookmark+ show each site's icon from Chrome's own icon store. It does not contact the websites or any third party to fetch them. A site you have never visited may show Chrome's default globe instead.

How does search work?

Start typing in the search box on the dashboard to find bookmarks by name, site or project. If you press Enter without choosing a bookmark, your search is passed to Chrome and runs in your own default search engine. That search engine's own privacy policy applies.

How do I remove Bookmark+?

Open chrome://extensions, find Bookmark+ and choose Remove. Its stored data is deleted from Chrome and your new tab goes back to normal. Any backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them.

Something is broken, or I have an idea

Please email contact@katachilabs.uk and tell us what happened. Bug reports and ideas from real use are very welcome.
